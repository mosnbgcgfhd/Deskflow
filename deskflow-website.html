Access, decided once

Exactly three roles. On purpose.

Custom permission builders are how access control quietly turns into a second job. DeskFlow ships with three roles that cover how a small company actually runs — nothing to configure, nothing to get wrong.

Admin

The person who set up the company. Usually one or two people.
  • ✓Everything managers can do
  • ✓Company settings
  • ✓Invite and remove employees

Manager

Runs projects and people, without touching the company account.
  • ✓Projects and tasks
  • ✓Team and reports
  • –Company settings

Employee

Focused on their own work and the projects they're part of.
  • ✓Their tasks
  • ✓Projects they're assigned to
  • –Reports and company settings
